How do we actually know where your town used to be?

Every star on every postcard comes from a real plate motion model — the same kind of computer model professional Earth scientists use, built by combining thousands of real measurements (the magnetic sea-floor stripes from Mission 2, matching rocks and fossils, and more) into one set of rules for how each plate has moved, year by year, for hundreds of millions of years. Punch in a place and a time, and the model can calculate exactly where that spot on Earth’s crust must have been sitting. It’s the same kind of model, called GPlates, that this whole suite is built on.
